# Synthetic campus demonstration

Use the existing development startup or the [release candidate](RELEASE_RUNBOOK.md), with explicit synthetic seeding. Use a disposable demonstration account and an administrator created through the secure CLI. Do not present these coordinates or observations as verified university infrastructure.

1. Open Explore campus. Choose Demo Welcome Centre and Demo Library, select the guest step-free preset, and plan a route. Compare the mapped line, distance, walking estimate and text directions. Inspect an alternative and compare A* with Dijkstra.
2. Toggle Avoid stairs and recalculate. Show how hard preferences change eligibility; narrow widths or unknown observations can honestly produce no route. Restore the original trip.
3. Register/sign in, save accessibility preferences and a place, reload, and show persistence on the dashboard. Sign out to show access protection.
4. As a normal user, submit an explicitly fictional concern related to Demo ramp, with a synthetic JPEG/PNG image. Its pending state does not close any pathway. Show owner history and private image access.
5. In a separate administrator session, review the concern with its current revision, identify affected physical edges and give a verification reason. In the navigation session, observe immediate route retirement and the updated route awaiting explicit confirmation. Confirm it before using the guidance.
6. Show database-backed analytics and their text/table alternatives. Resolve the verified concern with a reason; baseline availability and any other active hazard restrictions remain respected. A stale review is rejected and preserves the draft.
7. Open Image analysis. Explain its current unavailable state: no approved dataset/evaluated hazard weights exist. Optional synthetic pipeline and annotation-review fixtures prove execution/isolation only; they are not hazard accuracy or operational predictions.
8. For an operations demonstration, run strict HTTPS smoke checks and the coordinated restore verifier on its own fixture. Show the passing account/private-image/graph persistence summary without displaying credentials, private backup contents or real user evidence.

The real browser suites automate these application flows. [Testing](TESTING.md) and phase evidence document exact executed checks and their limits.
